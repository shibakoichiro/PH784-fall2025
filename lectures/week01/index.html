Week 1: Intro & Measures of Health Disparities

🎯 Learning Objectives

By the end of this session, you should be able to:

  • Classify key typology of social epidemiologic inquiries and their purposes
  • Explain the importance of questioning the research questions based on theories
  • Select measures of health disparity based on their pros and cons
  • Critically evaluate the literature that reports the magnitudes of health disparities

📅 Date and Time

Monday, September 8, 2025 | 10:00 AM–12:50 PM
Location: Boston University School of Public Health (CTC 460)

🗣️ Lecture Overview

This session begins with a brief overview of course objectives, a working agreement for the course, and introductions to social epidemiologic inquiries. We will then review the major concepts and methods in descriptively measuring health disparities.

📚 Required Readings

  • Braveman P. Health disparities and health equity: concepts and measurement. (2006) Annu Rev Public Health, 27:167-194.
  • Harper S, Lynch J. Health inequalities: measurement and decomposition. Methods in social epidemiology. 2017 Mar 20;2.
    • PDF version available at SSRN: https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=2887311
    • A comprehensive overview of health disparity metrics

📖 Optional Readings

  • Fox MP, Murray EJ, Lesko CR, Sealy-Jefferson S. On the need to revitalize descriptive epidemiology. American journal of epidemiology. 2022 Jul;191(7):1174-9.
  • Mackenbach, & Kunst. (1997). Measuring the magnitude of socio-economic inequalities in health: An overview of available measures illustrated with two examples from Europe. Social Science & Medicine, 44(6), 757-771.
    • This is a real-world application of SII/RII for assessing health disparities.
